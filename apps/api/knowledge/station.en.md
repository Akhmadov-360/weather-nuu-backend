# The NUUz air station

Weather NUUz (weather.nuu.uz) is a web platform of the National University of Uzbekistan (Tashkent) that collects and shows air readings. It is a diploma project: aggregation and spatial visualization of environmental indicators of urban air.

## Two measuring kits

The **Room** kit stands in the auditorium, next to the air conditioner. It measures temperature, humidity, pressure, dust (PM2.5 and PM10) and two gas estimates (MQ-5, MQ-3). The **Street** kit hangs outside the window. It measures temperature, humidity, pressure and the same two gas estimates, and has no dust sensor, so there is no dust reading or AQI for the street, and room dust cannot be compared with street dust.

## What the website offers

The dashboard shows the latest readings with colour zones, charts for any period (6 hours, 24 hours, up to several weeks), the AQI category of the room dust, and an export of the data. The assistant in the corner of the page answers questions about the current readings, about periods and about the meaning of the norms; its numbers always come from the sensors, never from memory.

## Limits to keep in mind

The kits are low-cost, so readings are estimates. The room reflects people, the air conditioner, cleaning and open windows. The assistant gives general information and is not a source of medical advice.
